01 / The Federation

Institutional overview

An international framework for exceptional threats.

The International Federation for Combating Terrorism operates through multilateral security cooperation established across successive international frameworks.

Federation activity is directed toward threats whose scale, complexity or jurisdictional reach exceeds the effective capacity of conventional mechanisms acting independently.

Public information is limited to material necessary for institutional transparency, legal notice and international coordination.

Public disclosure Operational structure is not published.

Information concerning personnel, facilities, deployments, technical capabilities and internal command architecture is not made available through this portal.

02 / Mandate

Authority

Threats do not become less serious at a border.

Transnational threats

Coordination where affected jurisdictions, infrastructure or populations extend across national boundaries.

Critical continuity

Support for continuity of essential systems where disruption presents severe international security consequences.

Exceptional response

Specialized capabilities may be coordinated when conventional measures are assessed as insufficient.

03 / International Cooperation

Cooperation

Independent jurisdictions.
Shared consequence.

The Federation maintains channels for authorized cooperation with competent public authorities and recognized international mechanisms.

Participation in a Federation-coordinated response does not transfer general sovereign authority. Operational arrangements are determined under the applicable international framework.
